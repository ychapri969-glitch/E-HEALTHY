Project Mission

E-HEALTHY (Eat Healthy) aims to eradicate campus nutritional deficiencies and lifestyle risks through instant AI face biometrics and Gemini AI counseling.

Tech Stack

Built with Tailwind CSS, Chart.js, Python Flask backend simulation, OpenCV face mesh models, and real-time integration with the Gemini API (`gemini-3-flash-preview`).

Why "Eat Healthy"?

Nutrition is medicine. Our platform emphasizes wholesome, whole-food eating habits tailored precisely to what your biometric scan and Gemini chat detect.
